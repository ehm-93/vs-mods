# Pemmican

## Jerky

Create a smoking rack from sticks, knife, and rope
Right click on a fire to make a smoking station, just like cooking pot + fire
Smoking station holds 16 meat (red, bush, fish, poultry)
Cook for 24 hours to make Jerky
Fuel lasts longer since it's a low temp fire or something idk w/e

smoked meat is preserved and can be eaten or used in stews directly

## Jerky Powder

rock + jerky -> jerky powder

can be used same as jerky if desired

## Pemmican

In cooking pot, 1 rendered fat + 1 Jerky Powder + optional 1 Jerky Powder (more nutrient) + optional 1 salt (preserve longer)

Store in crocks, seal to preserve longer (behaves same as stew)
