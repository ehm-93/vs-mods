# Clamp and Updraft Kiln

Add intermediate clay firing structures between "hole in the ground" and "massive and expensive beehive kiln", the clamp and updraft kilns.

## Clamp

Basically a charcoal mound but for firing clay instead of carbonizing.

## Updraft Kiln

A permanent kiln not unlike the beehive kiln but smaller (2x2x2 instead of 3x3x3) and made of fire clay instead of refractory bricks.
