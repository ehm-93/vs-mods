# Clamp and Updraft Kiln

Add intermediate clay firing structures between "hole in the ground" and "massive and expensive beehive kiln", the clamp and updraft kilns.

## Clamp

Basically a charcoal mound but for firing clay instead of carbonizing. I think you should only be able to use this for bricks, since they're the only clay thing you can stack unfired, and the pit kiln isn't that bad for small batches of other items. Looking for a loose approximation of a brick clamp, not an intricately stacked and layered construct of unfired brick and fuel.

Stack the dried, unfired bricks into a contiguous multi-block stack. Probably have some maximum size. Depending on size, require a certain amount of fuel. Maybe measured in burn duration? Different fuels provide their respective value, in seconds. Maybe 4 firewood worth per cube, so 100 seconds I think. Apply the fuel to the whole structure, not just the target cube so you can get it to the hidden blocks.

Cover the clamp in dirt or whatever, then light it exactly the same as a charcoal mound. Wait however long, depends on size, then enjoy your fired bricks. 

## Updraft Kiln

A permanent kiln not unlike the beehive kiln but smaller and made of fire clay instead of refractory bricks.

Flow (bloomery-inspired):

1. **Kiln base** — grid recipe from fired bricks (same spirit as the bloomery's recipe) makes a placeable "kiln base" block. Use 1 base for a 1x1 kiln, or place 4 in a 2x2 square for the large kiln.
2. **Construct with fireclay bricks** — right-click a placed base with fireclay bricks. A ghost outline of the finished kiln appears; block info shows how many more bricks are needed. Keep right-clicking to build it up.
   - 1x1: a single interior cube to fill with clayware.
   - 2x2: two interior layers of 2x2, supported by a central pillar with a shelf for the second layer.
3. **Door** — the face toward the player when the last base was placed stays open with an inset doorway. Load unfired clayware through it, then right-click with more fireclay bricks to seal the door.
4. **Fire** — right-click with fuel (firewood, peat, coals, coke) to fuel, light with a torch — both just like a bloomery. The kiln sparks up, cooks for a while, then cools a little longer.
5. **Unload** — right-click to break the door open and take the fired pottery out.
