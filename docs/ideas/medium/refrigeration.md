# Refrigeration

Different tiers of refrigeration.

## Zeer pot

A pot-in-pot refrigerator. Basically a vessel with a layer of wet sand. The water evaporates and draws heat from the inner pot.

## Ice house

Put ice in this structure in your cellar to give a preservation bonus to all containers. 

## Ammonia refrigeration loop

A primitive but functional take on a modern refrigerant loop. Likely needs some of the fun from [chemistry](../hard/petrochem.md).
